    2026-27 Rule Changes

    Announced June 29, 2026, following the Board of Governors meeting (St. Petersburg, FL). These matter for broadcast and game notes this season.

    Three-point standings system

    Regulation win = 3 pts · OT/SO win = 2 pts · OT/SO loss = 1 pt. Standings and points-pace math change accordingly.

    Overtime

    Extended from 5 to 7 minutes, still sudden-death 3-on-3. Still tied → shootout.

    Shootout

    Initial round cut from 5 to 3 shooters. If tied after 3, sudden death — every eligible skater must shoot before anyone repeats (previously repeats allowed after round of 5).

    Presidents Cup Playoffs

    Top 8 teams qualify. All rounds best-of-five.

    Diving / embellishment

    If the offended player embellishes after a penalty signal, only the diving/embellishment minor is assessed — the original call is nullified.

    Instant replay — coach's challenge

    In replay-equipped venues: one challenge per team per game; unsuccessful = delay-of-game minor. Reviewable: puck over goal line · distinct kicking motion · goal before frame dislodged · puck directed/batted/thrown in by attacker.
